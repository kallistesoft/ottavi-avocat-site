Canadian immigration

Vanessa Poughon

A French-Canadian lawyer with nine years of experience in criminal and immigration law in France, Vanessa Poughon continued her legal career in Canada by being admitted to the Barreau du Québec.

Now based in Alberta after living six years in Québec, she helps individuals and families make their Canadian immigration plans a reality.

Through her firm POUGHON IMMIGRATION, she offers strategic, rigorous and human legal support in Canadian immigration law.

Her areas of focus are federal and provincial immigration programs, including Express Entry and Francophone Mobility.

Legal support for your immigration project in Canada, from coast to coast.
